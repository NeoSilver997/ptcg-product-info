# Pokemon TCG Advanced Rules Instructions

This document contains the official Pokemon Trading Card Game advanced rules and detailed clarifications from the official advanced manual.

## I. Game Rules Supplement

### A. Pokemon Cards

#### A-01 Attacks
- Players can only use 1 attack from their Active Pokemon per turn
- Must have required energy attached to use attacks
- Energy remains attached after attack use
- Turn ends after attack is used (unless specified otherwise)

#### A-02 Abilities
- Some Pokemon have "Abilities" in addition to attacks
- Two types: declared abilities (player chooses when to use) and automatic abilities (always active when Pokemon is in play)
- Declared abilities: Player must announce use, follow conditions
- Automatic abilities: Always active, cannot be turned off by player

#### A-03 Retreat
- Players can retreat their Active Pokemon to Bench
- Must discard energy equal to retreat cost shown on card
- Can only retreat once per turn
- Pokemon with no retreat cost marker don't discard energy
- Cannot retreat if insufficient energy or no Benched Pokemon available

#### A-04 Placing on Bench
- Can place Basic Pokemon from hand to Bench
- Unlimited placements per turn as long as Bench space available
- Place one Pokemon at a time

#### A-05 Evolution
- Can evolve by placing evolution card from hand onto eligible Pokemon
- Works on both Active and Benched Pokemon
- Cannot evolve same turn Pokemon is played or already evolved
- Cannot evolve on either player's first turn
- Evolved Pokemon keeps damage counters and attached cards
- Special states and effects are removed upon evolution

### B. Trainer Cards

#### B-01 Item Cards
- Can use unlimited Item cards per turn
- Discard after use
- Marked with "Item cards can be used any number of times during your turn"

#### B-02 Supporter Cards
- Can use 1 Supporter card per turn
- Discard after use
- Cannot use on first player's first turn
- Marked with "Supporter cards can be used once per turn"

#### B-03 Stadium Cards
- Place one Stadium card per turn
- Remains in play until replaced by another Stadium card
- Cannot play if same name Stadium already in play
- Both players affected by Stadium effects

#### B-04 ACE SPEC Cards
- Only 1 ACE SPEC card allowed per deck total (cannot mix different ACE SPEC cards)


### C. Energy Cards
- Basic Energy: 9 types (no Colorless Basic Energy)
- Special Energy: Have additional effects
- Attach up to 1 Energy per turn
- No card limit for Basic Energy in deck

### D. Knocked Out (KO)
- Pokemon with damage ≥ HP is KO'd
- Attacking player takes Prize card
- KO'd Pokemon and attached cards go to discard
- Replace Active Pokemon from Bench

### E. Win/Loss Conditions
- Take all 6 Prize cards to win
- No Pokemon left on field = loss
- Cannot draw card at turn start due to empty deck = loss

### F. Pokemon Check
- Occurs at end of each turn
- Check all Pokemon for special conditions
- Process order: Poison → Burn → Sleep → Paralyze
- Also check Abilities and Trainer card effects that trigger during Pokemon Check

### G. Battle Setup
1. Greet and rock-paper-scissors for first turn
2. Shuffle decks
3. Draw 7 cards
4. Mulligan if no Basic Pokemon
5. Place Active Pokemon (face down initially)
6. Place up to 5 Bench Pokemon
7. Place 6 Prize cards
8. Reveal Pokemon and begin battle

### H. Deck
- Select from deck: Look at top cards, choose specified number
- Shuffle deck after viewing (unless specified otherwise)

## II. Card Text Explanations

### A. About Card Text
- Card text takes precedence over rulebook
- "Cannot do X" takes precedence over "does X" effects

### B. Damage Calculation Terms

#### B-01 +X damage
- Add to base attack damage
- Calculated in attack damage step

#### B-02 X×Y damage
- Multiply condition count by damage number
- Calculated in attack damage step

#### B-03 -X damage
- Subtract from base attack damage
- Calculated in attack damage step

#### B-04 Damage "+X"
- Add damage after base calculation
- Applied in attacker's effect step

#### B-05 Damage "-X"
- Subtract damage after base calculation
- Applied in defender's effect step

#### B-06 Ignore Weakness/Resistance
- Skip weakness/resistance calculation
- Only affects Active Pokemon

#### B-07 Ignore additional effects
- Skip defender's additional effects
- Does not ignore "cannot be damaged" effects

#### B-08 X Pokemon on opponent's field each take Y damage
- Choose X opponent's Pokemon, each takes Y damage
- Weakness/resistance not calculated for Bench Pokemon

#### B-09 This Pokemon also takes X damage
- Attacking Pokemon takes recoil damage

## Important Notes for Database Development

### Card Relationships
- Evolution chains must be properly linked
- Energy requirements must match attack costs
- Weakness/resistance data must be accurate
- Special card rules (ex, Ancient/Future) must be flagged

### Data Validation
- Deck construction rules (60 cards, evolution requirements, max 4 copies of same non-basic energy card by name, unlimited basic energy cards, only 1 ACE SPEC card total per deck)
- Attack legality (energy costs, turn restrictions)
- Effect interactions (status conditions, damage modifiers)

### Game State Tracking
- Active/Bench positions
- Damage counters
- Status conditions
- Energy attachments
- Prize card counts

This advanced rules reference provides comprehensive clarifications for implementing game logic, validating card data, and ensuring database accuracy for Pokemon TCG applications.
